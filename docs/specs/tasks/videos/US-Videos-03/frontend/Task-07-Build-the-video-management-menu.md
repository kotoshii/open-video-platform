## Build the video management menu

Needs: [US-Channels-04 Task-04 — Build the channel video list](../../../channels/US-Channels-04/frontend/Task-04-Build-the-channel-video-list.md)

Add the 3-dot button to the author's own videos in the channel list — on hover on desktop, always visible on mobile —
opening a menu with exactly three items: Edit, Change visibility, Delete.

The same menu opens from the edit button under the player on the author's own video page
([US-Videos-01](../../../../user-stories/videos/US-Videos-01-Watch-videos.md)).

Why: the menu is one component with three entry points, so managing a video from the watch page and from the channel
page cannot behave differently.
